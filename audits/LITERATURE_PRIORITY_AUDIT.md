## Closest prior result

Kozerenko's 2019 theorem gives a multigraph realization under essentially the same positive-imbalance source hypothesis. Allowing the target to be a multigraph is weaker and does not imply realization by a finite simple graph. The 2023 source explicitly distinguishes the multigraph theorem from the still-open simple-graph conjecture.

The arbitrary-subset form of the Erdos-Gallai criterion was already public. The apparently new content is the source-graph theorem forcing that inequality for every `k`-edge set whose members all have imbalance at least `k`.

## Residual source gaps

The audit could not eliminate every possibility of unpublished, unindexed, or inaccessible overlap. Recorded gaps include an ambiguous flattened 2025 author-profile listing, one unidentified forward citation, incomplete subscription-database coverage, and a nonverifiable AI-summary snippet. Those gaps justify moderate rather than high novelty confidence.
