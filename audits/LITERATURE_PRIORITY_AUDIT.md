## Closest prior result

Kozerenko's 2019 theorem gives a multigraph realization under essentially the same positive-imbalance source hypothesis. Allowing the target to be a multigraph is weaker and does not imply realization by a finite simple graph. The 2023 source explicitly distinguishes the multigraph theorem from the still-open simple-graph conjecture.

The arbitrary-subset form of the Erdős--Gallai criterion was already public.
Once the imbalance multiset is known to be graphical, the corresponding
inequality holds for every subset. Schreib also formalizes an all-subset
statement. The threshold-set theorem remains a useful directly proved lemma in
the alternative argument here, but it is not a stronger headline result.
