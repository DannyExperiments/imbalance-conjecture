# Focused priority search for the extension results

**Search cutoff:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** targeted primary-source and DOI search; not an absolute-priority certificate

## Headline theorem

The original finite-simple locally-irregular Imbalance Conjecture was proved
earlier by A. A. Raoui:

- A. A. Raoui, *The Imbalance Conjecture*, Zenodo,
  [doi:10.5281/zenodo.20589431](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20589431),
  deposited 2026-06-08.

James Alexander Schreib later deposited a Lean 4 formalization that includes
the headline theorem and an Erdős--Gallai inequality for every edge subset:

- J. A. Schreib, Zenodo,
  [doi:10.5281/zenodo.21542164](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21542164),
  deposited 2026-07-25.

Accordingly, neither the Imbalance Theorem nor bare threshold-set
nonnegativity is claimed as new in this repository.

## Focused extension comparison

The search compared the present manuscript against the Raoui and Schreib
records and searched primary-source/DOI literature for the following four
claims:

1. explicit profile-sensitive Erdős--Gallai deficit bounds;
2. the source-graph equality classification by stars and once-subdivided
   stars;
3. exact zero-edge correction inequalities; and
4. graphicality when at most one source edge joins equal-degree vertices.

No earlier statement of these four results was located through the cutoff.
The one-equal-edge theorem is substantially broader than the antiregular-graph
case in Kozerenko--Serdiuk, *Opuscula Mathematica* 43 (2023), 81--96,
[doi:10.7494/OpMath.2023.43.1.81](https://doi.org/10.7494/OpMath.2023.43.1.81).
