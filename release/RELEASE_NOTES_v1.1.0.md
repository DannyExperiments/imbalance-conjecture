# Release notes v1.1.0

This release corrects the paper's historical and novelty framing after locating
Raoui's earlier proof (DOI `10.5281/zenodo.20589431`) and Schreib's Lean 4
formalization (DOI `10.5281/zenodo.21542164`).

The mathematical proof is retained as a later independent argument. The paper
now foregrounds its different first-failed-index/head-reserve method,
profile-sensitive deficit bounds, complete threshold-equality classification,
and one-equal-edge extension. It makes no priority claim for the headline
theorem or threshold-set nonnegativity, and makes no absolute novelty claim for
the additional results pending a refreshed focused search.

Version `v1.0.0` remains available only as a superseded historical snapshot.
